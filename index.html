Roadmaps are promises. Now they have receipts.

Crypto teams put each roadmap promise on-chain, with a deadline and their own official page as evidence. When it is time, GenLayer validators read that page and agree on one verdict: kept or broken. Every verdict builds the project's public Trust Score.

How a verdict is reached

Lock

The team writes the promise, the deadline and its official page. Its wallet is recorded as the author.

Read

Anyone presses Verify. Each validator opens the live page itself.

Agree

An AI judge on every validator decides delivered or not, and the verdicts must match.

Settle

Kept or broken is saved on-chain and updates the Trust Score. Nobody can edit it later.
